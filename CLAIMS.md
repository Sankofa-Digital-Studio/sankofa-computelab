# Learner claims and review evidence

This first hourglass pass starts with six broad learner promises, narrows them to one overview disclosure, and checks the result across six real routes on a 390 × 844 Chromium viewport.

| Claim | Evidence in the app | Remaining approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curriculum coverage | Mastery pathway presents selected level-specific practice | Teaching approver to validate scope against the current syllabus |
| Marking | Practice Hub provides timed quiz scoring and practical self-marking | Teaching approver to validate assessment language |
| Evidence | Assignments describes local receipts and attachments | Product approver to confirm the classroom handoff |
| Progress | My Progress displays history saved by this browser | Product approver to confirm the storage wording |
| Lecturer review | Feedback identifies the same-device message boundary | Product approver to confirm support arrangements |
| Notifications | College Notices identifies local device testing and the live delivery dependency | Product approver to confirm delivery scope |

The production preview opened all six destinations from the disclosure. This checks navigation and discoverability. Submission, persistence and lecturer review require their own end-to-end evidence. Sign-off and site-wide point-of-use copy review remain open.
