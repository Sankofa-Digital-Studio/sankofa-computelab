# Code of Conduct

ComputeLab welcomes contributors and learners of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, give specific and constructive feedback, credit work, and respect boundaries and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory or sexual remarks, threats, personal attacks, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable. These standards apply to repository interactions and public representations of the project.

Use GitHub's private reporting options for abusive content or accounts, or contact a repository maintainer through an established private channel. Keep identifying details out of public discussions. Maintainers review reports discreetly, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and may remove content, restrict participation, or refer serious threats to GitHub. Maintainers named in a report should recuse themselves.

[GitHub abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-abuse-reporting)
